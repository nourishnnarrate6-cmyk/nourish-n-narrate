Use Nourish N Narrate as an app

Add Nourish N Narrate to your home screen and it opens like a normal app — full screen, its own icon, no address bar, and your tracker works even with a patchy signal. It takes about ten seconds and installs nothing from an app store.

✅ You're already using the installed app — nothing more to do. Enjoy!
  1. 1
    Open this page in Safari This only works in Safari. If you arrived from Instagram, a text message or another app, tap the ⋯ or ↗ menu and choose Open in Safari first.
  2. 2
    Tap the Share button The square with an arrow pointing up ↑ — at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, or the top on an iPad.
  3. 3
    Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen” It sits below the row of sharing apps, next to a ＋ icon. You may need to scroll a little way down the list.
  4. 4
    Tap “Add” The leaf-and-book icon appears on your home screen. Open it from there and it runs full screen.
Heads up: iPhones don't show an automatic “install” pop-up for web apps — Apple doesn't offer one, so the Share menu is the only way in. You'll also need to sign in once inside the app, because iOS treats it as a separate browser.
  1. 1
    Open this page in Chrome If you tapped a link inside another app, choose Open in Chrome from that app's menu first.
  2. 2
    Tap the ⋮ menu, top right Or just use the Install app button above if it appeared — that does the whole thing in one tap.
  3. 3
    Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” The wording depends on your Chrome version — both do the same thing.
  4. 4
    Confirm with “Install” It lands on your home screen and in your app drawer, like any other app.
Chrome sometimes offers this by itself after you've used the site for a while. If you've dismissed that prompt before, it won't ask again — the ⋮ menu always works.
  1. 1
    Use Chrome or Edge Safari and Firefox on a computer can't install web apps yet.
  2. 2
    Look at the right of the address bar There's a small install icon — a screen with a downward arrow ⊕. Click it.
  3. 3
    Click “Install” Nourish N Narrate opens in its own window and pins to your dock, taskbar or Start menu.
No install icon? Open the ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app, or Apps → Install this site as an app.
📴 Works offline Recipes and your tracker layout load without a signal. Logs sync the moment you're back online.
⚡ Opens instantly The app is stored on your device, so there's no page load to wait through.
🗑️ Easy to remove Delete the icon like any app. Your account and logs stay safe in your profile.
